# Tried and still open

These are limited project observations reported by the owner and summarized for collaborators, not a public reproducible benchmark. Private working files and captures are not included. Please do not turn a narrow observation into a general cause or fix claim.

## Camera
- A hash-only camera trial did not resolve the user's observed problem.
- Source anchor coverage fitting the notes does not prove that the engine will frame the intended frets correctly.
- Open: isolate supported metadata and transition effects with controlled, original synthetic cases and actual engine observations.

## Pause, resume and seeking
- Successful cold-decoder seeking does not establish correct warm, already-running game behaviour.
- A contrast in seek-table presence is confounded; it does not establish causation.
- Open: repeatable cold/warm and uninterrupted/pause-resume comparisons, with explicit clocks, controls and uncertainty.

## Public fixtures
[Synthetic source inputs v0.1](https://github.com/seantheholy101-glitch/chartbot-collaboration/releases/tag/v0.1-synthetic-inputs) are available.
- High-fret: phrase CSV and metronome only; conversion, import and in-game camera validation are pending.
- Timing: 10 scheduled synthetic events with a deliberately 80 ms-late second-half variant. Scheduled starts are not perceptual-onset annotations. Independent analysis/correction and listener validation are pending; this is not validation of Space Cadet.
- Tone: the My Happiness-inspired method challenge remains open, with no tone audio fixture supplied.

There are playable, user-tested arrangements, but no general transcription-accuracy or autonomous-transcription claim. No engine or perceptual-quality result is claimed for the public inputs.
